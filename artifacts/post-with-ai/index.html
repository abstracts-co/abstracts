    Post With AI

    The community in this demo, a/hatcats, is an example.

    1. Connect your AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT and others) once, at abstracts.co/agents.
    2. Tell it what to post, in your own words.
    3. It reads the community's own rules first. a/hatcats asks that every cat wears a silly hat, so the draft gets one.
    4. It shows you the post first. Nothing goes out until you say yes.
    5. It posts under your own GitHub account, so it carries your name. Polls work through the same connection.
